Wairimu Dream Now NTSA Approved

Official news and instructional guides from our senior training team.

📅 28 Aug 2026 ✍️ Admissions Desk ⏱️ 3 min read
28
AUG

We are delighted to formally announce that Wairimu Dream Defensive Driving School has received official accreditation and full licensing from the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) under Kenya's modernized driving curriculum.

What This Means for Our Learners

NTSA accreditation ensures that every student who enrolls with us undergoes training that strictly meets national road safety standards. From theoretical classroom modules to model town board simulations and highway maneuvers, our syllabus is designed to produce skilled, disciplined, and defensive drivers.

  • Direct eCitizen & NTSA TIMS Integration: Seamless registration for your Provisional Driving License (PDL) and test booking without intermediaries.
  • Certified Master Instructors: All our driving tutors are NTSA-certified, vetted, and continually trained in the latest road safety protocols.
  • Transparent Testing & Issuance: Direct pipeline to official government testing centers with high first-time pass rates.

Our Commitment to Road Safety

With Kenya's growing highway infrastructure, defensive driving skills are more vital than ever. At Wairimu Dream, we don't just teach you how to pass the test; we instill habits that keep you, your passengers, and fellow road users safe for life.

Visit any of our branches in Muguga, Kiganjo, or Landless to inspect our facilities and start your 21-day journey to a genuine driving license!
