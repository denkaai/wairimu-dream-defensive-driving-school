OUR MISSION & HERITAGE

Leading the Standard in Kenyan Driver Education

Wairimu Dream Defensive Driving School was established with a singular vision: to drastically improve road safety in Kenya by transforming novice learners into confident, defensive motorists.

Fully accredited by the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA), we combine state-of-the-art Model Town Board theory training with rigorous, dual-pedal practical road driving across our Muguga, Kiganjo, and Landless branches.
